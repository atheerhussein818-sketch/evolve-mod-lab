## 🌱 What Is Evolution Studio?

Evolution Studio is a **modular training companion** designed for players who love the slow-burn satisfaction of raising, evolving, and refining virtual creatures. Instead of treating progression as a straight line, this project reframes it as a **living ecosystem** — every stat point is a seed, every battle a season, every evolution a metamorphosis.

Where classic trainer tools simply flip switches, Evolution Studio offers a **walled garden of possibilities**: fine-tuned multipliers, adaptive difficulty curves, growth timelines, and a feedback loop that learns from how you actually play.

Think of it as a gardener's toolkit for digital lifeforms — patient, deliberate, and endlessly rewarding.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 🎛️ Adaptive Growth Engine
A dynamic tuning layer that adjusts experience curves, stat ceilings, and evolution thresholds in real time. Rather than forcing a fixed pace, the engine observes your engagement rhythm and offers calibrated suggestions. Players who prefer a leisurely stroll and players who sprint through regions both get a curve that respects their tempo.

### 🌍 Responsive Interface
The dashboard reshapes itself across desktop, tablet, and handheld screens without a single pixel wasted. Panels dock, collapse, and rearrange intelligently, so your focus stays on the creatures — not the chrome.

### 🗣️ Multilingual Support
Fourteen locale packs ship out of the box, with community-contributed translations for dozens more. Every label, tooltip, and log entry respects your system language, and adding a new locale is as approachable as editing a single structured file.

### 🛰️ 24/7 Customer Support
A rotating support desk staffed across time zones keeps questions answered around the clock. Whether it's a configuration puzzle at 3 AM or a feature request during a lunch break, someone is always on the other end.

### 📊 Deep Telemetry Dashboard
Visualize growth trajectories, type-advantage heatmaps, and average time-to-evolution across your entire roster. Export snapshots as JSON or CSV for spreadsheet tinkerers.

### 🧩 Plugin Architecture
Third-party developers can extend the suite with custom modules — new metrics, alternate UIs, or region-specific overrides — all loaded through a sandboxed registry.

### 🔐 Local-First Privacy
Your save data never leaves your machine unless you explicitly export it. No accounts, no telemetry beacons, no cloud tethering.

### 🕰️ Timeline Rewind
Snapshots are captured automatically before every major change, letting you roll back to any earlier state with a single click. Mistaken tweaks are a thing of the past.

### 🎨 Theming & Presets
Choose from curated visual themes or craft your own palettes. Save entire configuration profiles and switch between them per save file.

## 🧠 Design Philosophy

Most trainer utilities are built like vending machines — insert a desire, receive an outcome. Evolution Studio is built like a **greenhouse**. It provides soil, light, and water in the right proportions, then lets the organism do what organisms do: grow, adapt, and occasionally surprise you.

This distinction matters. When a tool respects the underlying game's design, the result feels like **collaboration** rather than substitution. Every slider is bounded by sensible limits; every feature is documented with the reasoning behind it. Nothing here tries to erase the challenge — it simply gives you a better set of gloves while you garden.

## 🚀 Getting Started

The suite is distributed as a portable, self-contained bundle. Unpack it anywhere on your filesystem, launch the bootstrap executable for your platform, and the first-run wizard walks you through folder detection, profile setup, and optional cloud-save linking.

For users who prefer a guided flow, the in-app onboarding prompts appear automatically on launch and cover everything from initial tuning to advanced plugin management in under ten minutes.

Developers wishing to build from source can find the toolchain manifest at the repository root. The build pipeline is standardized across all supported operating systems and requires only a modern runtime environment.

## 🗂️ Project Structure

The repository is organized around a clean separation of concerns:

- **core/** — the adaptive engine, growth models, and snapshot system
- **ui/** — responsive dashboards, themes, and accessibility layers
- **locales/** — every language pack, versioned alongside the release train
- **plugins/** — bundled sample modules and the sandboxed loader
- **docs/** — architecture notes, contribution guides, and design rationale
- **tools/** — helper scripts for packaging, translation sync, and QA

Each directory contains its own focused documentation, so contributors can dive into a single concern without reading the entire codebase.
